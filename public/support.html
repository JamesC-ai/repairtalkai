Make the reflection specific and private.

Formatting a conversation

  1. Put each message or speaking turn on its own line.
  2. Use neutral labels such as “A:” and “B:” instead of full names.
  3. Remove addresses, workplaces, account information, and unrelated personal details.
  4. Include enough surrounding text to review a phrase in context.
  5. Use the structured fields to describe one observable event and one doable request.

A match looks wrong

Pattern matching is deliberately simple and cautious. A quoted phrase, joke, historical account, or isolated word can be misclassified. Read every match in context and ignore suggestions that do not fit.

Safety-related wording appears

The tool cannot assess danger. If you fear harm or retaliation, do not use the repair draft to plan a confrontation. Prioritize getting to a safer place and contacting local emergency services or someone you trust.
