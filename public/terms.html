Terms

A wording aid, not therapy, mediation, or a safety assessment.

RepairTalkAI identifies listed language patterns and produces informational drafts. Matches can be false positives, miss context, or reflect normal wording rather than a relationship pattern. The service does not determine who is right.

No diagnosis or professional relationship

RepairTalkAI does not diagnose abuse, coercive control, mental illness, personality traits, deception, or relationship compatibility. It does not create a therapist-client, attorney-client, mediator, or emergency-service relationship.

Safety limitations

Software cannot determine whether a person is in immediate danger. Do not rely on a “no safety phrase detected” result. If you fear harm, retaliation, surveillance, or being prevented from leaving, prioritize getting to a safer place and contacting local emergency services or a trusted person rather than using a joint repair script.

Customer-provided text

You must have permission or a lawful basis to submit conversation text for a paid review. Remove unnecessary identifying information. Do not use the service to harass, manipulate, impersonate, monitor, or pressure another person.

Prepared services

Scope, delivery timing, revisions, and refund eligibility are confirmed after checkout. We may decline material that cannot be handled safely or within the stated scope.
